Folded calendars: a calendar can fold its back-to-back events into one block per day, like a school day of lessons. Tap the block to see them, or use Show all events in the View menu. Events marked Always show, one-off events and titles with words you pick stay visible. Each calendar can be shown, folded or hidden from the View menu. Widgets now open a settings screen when added: pick the calendars they show and whether folded calendars stay folded.
