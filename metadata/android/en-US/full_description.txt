OpenCal is a calendar that keeps your data on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking. If you want your events on more than one device, you can sync them through a small server you run yourself.

<b>Views and widgets</b>
Schedule, Day, Week and Month views, with swipe navigation and a header that tells you where you are. Four home-screen widgets (Schedule, Today, Week, Month) that follow the app's colors and light/dark theme.

<b>Adding events quickly</b>
• Quick Add: type "Lunch with John Fri 1pm at Cafe X" and review the parsed event before saving.
• Stamps: save events you repeat and drop them on any empty time slot in one tap.
• Birthdays: add a name and date of birth and see "Dana's 36th birthday" every year.

<b>Repeats, reminders and time zones</b>
• Repeating events, with pauses that skip a date range and resume on their own.
• Reminders down to the minute, with presets and custom values. Reminders are scheduled on the device and need no Google services.
• Quiet time and Do Not Disturb, scheduled like events: quiet delivers reminders silently, Do Not Disturb skips them.
• Floating or fixed time: a 9:00 run can stay at 9:00 wherever you travel, while a fixed meeting keeps the same moment and its time zone.

<b>Organizing</b>
Calendars with their own default reminders, repeat, location and tags. 20 named colors, a color wheel and your own saved colors. Color rules that color every event whose title contains a word. Multi-select in Day and Week view to move or delete several events together, with Undo.

<b>Your data stays yours</b>
• Everything is stored locally on the device.
• Back up all your data to one file and restore it on another phone.
• Import and export standard .ics files to move events to and from other calendar apps.
• Optional self-hosted sync: run the OpenCal server at home (one Docker command), enter its address and key in Settings, and your phones stay in step.
• Optional AI assistant tools over MCP on the same server, so an assistant can read your schedule and add or move events, with every change undoable.

OpenCal is open source (MIT). The F-Droid build contains no proprietary libraries and never loads code from the network.
